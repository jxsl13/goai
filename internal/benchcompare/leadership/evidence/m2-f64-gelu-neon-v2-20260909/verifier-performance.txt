Limitations
-----------
This is a frozen-binary microbenchmark comparison on one Apple M2 Pro, one OS
and Go 1.27.1 session. Count seven limits power and precision. The declared
exact rank test is an unpaired label-permutation Mann-Whitney-style test even
though execution was order-balanced in pairs; it does not model pair effects.
Benchmark iterations are adaptively chosen. Warmups were excluded and their raw
measurements were not retained. The environment snapshot disclosed intermittent
background activity (including a VM and system services); there was no continuous
CPU-frequency, thermal, or load trace, CPU affinity, or cross-day replication.
The raw logs demonstrate invocation order and PASS, not semantic equivalence;
correctness/codegen qualification is covered by the separate verifier report.

Performance-only conclusion
---------------------------
REJECT. The frozen V2 candidate satisfies every one of the 24 per-campaign large
public target gates with wide margin and p<0.05, eliminates rather than repeats
the rejected V1 small active-forward regression, shows no significant >3% small
or fixed-control time regression, and shows no allocs/op median increase.
Nevertheless, the fixed Softplus GMP12 B/op median increases in all 3 campaigns
(and SiLUBackward in 2/3), which is reproducible allocation-byte movement and
therefore vetoes promotion under the unrelaxed assigned gate. Statistical
nonsignificance does not erase a separately required repeated-median veto. This
result supports only this internal frozen-binary qualification; it makes no
external-library, end-to-end model, or hardware leadership claim.
