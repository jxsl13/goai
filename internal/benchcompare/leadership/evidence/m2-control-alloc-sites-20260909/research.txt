R-01M23VWNB5FRP — prospective allocation-site attribution research
Date: 2026-09-09

Conclusion and boundary
=======================

The completed fixed-N diagnostic is sufficient to motivate, but not to supply,
site attribution. Its 504 valid N=1024 records show exact old/old variability and
repeated old/V2 parallel Softplus median paired deltas of +3,264/+3,424/+10,864
bytes and +2/+9/+27 allocations. Those are observations, not a candidate-caused
effect. A minimal follow-up should profile Softplus only, separately at
GOMAXPROCS=1 and 12, with contemporaneous old/old and old/V2 pairs. It must use
matched test-only instrumentation and retain both immediate process-wide
MemStats deltas and per-site allocation-profile deltas. No result changes either
V2 rejection or ARM64-F64-GELU-CONTROLS-001.

Observed source paths and potential allocation sites
====================================================

The old and V2 benchmark callbacks and shared paths are byte-identical. A bounded
`git diff --exit-code dd1e779e... 361955ac... --` over bench_test.go,
activation_bwd_f64_test.go, execute.go, cpu.go, tensor.go, storage.go, and
allocator.go returned no diff. The actual runtime delta changes only GELU gates
in backend/cpu/activation_bwd_f64.go:43-54 and elementwise.go:276-289, adds the
ARM64 GELU implementation and package variables at
backend/cpu/vgelu_f64_arm64.go:1-80, and adjusts architecture gate declarations.
The new variables initialize before benchmark timing; no V2 source calls a
control callback. They can change whole-process initialization/layout, but any
downstream allocation effect is a hypothesis, not an observed control path.

The exact control chain is:

* backend/cpu/bench_test.go:18-28 constructs backend/context outside ResetTimer,
  then calls backend.Execute once per iteration. Sigmoid and 256K Softplus are at
  lines 48-58. backend/cpu/activation_bwd_f64_test.go:54-76 does the analogous
  setup for 256K SiLU backward.
* backend/execute.go:180-325 resolves a cached kernel and calls it at line 309.
  The first cache miss can allocate a Context and resolvedDispatch at lines
  111-170, but the fixed-count diagnostic's one-iteration calibration normally
  warms this before its reported run. This is observed source behavior; whether
  any object escapes in a particular build was not tested here.
* Inputs are already contiguous, so Tensor.Contiguous returns the input at
  tensor/tensor.go:498-505. Sigmoid allocates its output at
  backend/cpu/elementwise.go:515-517; Softplus does so at lines 624-632. SiLU
  backward allocates an output and a same-sized sigmoid scratch at
  backend/cpu/activation_bwd_f64.go:63-76. Each returns a one-element tensor
  slice. These are direct candidate sites: tensor.NewOn creates a tensorBlock and
  Storage at tensor/tensor.go:65-96; newStorageWith selects the typed F64 path at
  tensor/storage.go:32-56; heapAllocator.allocF64 makes the data slice at
  tensor/allocator.go:59-64. Return-slice literals are also potential sites.
* All three kernels call parallel. backend/cpu/cpu.go:265-375 creates a per-call
  poolBarrier, passes its pointer and the captured body in poolTask values, and
  either sends, steals/drains, runs inline, or waits. Persistent workers execute
  tasks at lines 214-258. Channels and goroutines are created once at lines
  190-205. The closure, escaping barrier, wait/scheduler bookkeeping, worker
  stack growth, and runtime profiling bookkeeping are potential sources; source
  alone does not prove which allocate or how often. At GOMAXPROCS=1 the body is
  invoked inline at lines 266-270.

Thus output/storage allocation is expected on every iteration; SiLU also has
scratch. Allocation variability beyond those stable sites could arise from the
parallel ownership/scheduler path or other goroutines in the Go process. External
OS activity can affect scheduling and GC timing, but it does not itself allocate
objects in the Go heap.

Pinned Go 1.27.1 semantics and limits
=====================================

testing/benchmark.go:138-183 snapshots process-wide MemStats.Mallocs and
TotalAlloc at StartTimer/StopTimer/ResetTimer. runN forces GC, then brackets the
callback at lines 196-227. testing.Benchmark first runs N=1 at lines 229-268 and
then the fixed count at lines 327-362; BenchmarkResult stores aggregate totals,
and per-op values are integer division by N at lines 535-590. Consequently the
completed diagnostic's totals cover the final timed callback only, whereas a
whole-process allocation profile around testing.Benchmark would also include its
calibration and harness.

runtime/mstats.go:54-91 defines TotalAlloc and Mallocs as cumulative Go-heap
counters; ReadMemStats is current and stop-the-world at lines 350-365. These
counters have no goroutine/site filter.

runtime/pprof/pprof.go:123-142 says heap/allocs profiles contain allocation sites
since process start but publish a GC-stable snapshot; allocs merely defaults the
display to cumulative alloc_space. The protobuf contains alloc_objects,
alloc_space, inuse_objects, and inuse_space
(runtime/pprof/protomem.go:15-60). At the default rate, samples are Poisson-scaled
by allocation size (lines 63-85), so they are estimates. runtime.MemProfileRate=1
requests every allocated block and must be set once, as early as possible
(runtime/mprof.go:856-870); malloc.go:1333-1336 and 2218-2248 show the sampling
and stack-recording path, which is material perturbation.

Profiles are cumulative snapshots, not timed-region deltas. runtime.MemProfile
can lag by up to two GC cycles (runtime/mprof.go:905-925), because allocation and
free events are staged in future cycles (lines 114-143, 432-472). In this pinned
runtime, an explicit runtime.GC waits through sweep and publishes the completed
snapshot before returning (runtime/mgc.go:519-591); the experiment must use that
boundary rather than an arbitrary sleep. Raw runtime.MemProfile records expose
only 32 PCs; standard pprof uses the configured profiler stack depth (default
128, runtime/extern.go:167-170). Fully freed sites require inuseZero=true.

Rate 1 removes statistical sampling for allocations made after it takes effect,
but does not make the profile identical to BenchmarkResult totals: profiling
changes scheduling/GC, records allocation-slot size, may truncate/aggregate
stacks, can omit rare runtime initialization cases, and the profile publication
window includes the forced GC. Profile writing itself allocates. Therefore no
profile sum may be asserted equal to the immediate MemStats delta, and profiled
results cannot be substituted for the unprofiled completed evidence.

Bounded prospective matched experiment
======================================

Instrumentation. Add one byte-identical, opt-in `_test.go` file to both detached
pins; hash that file, both sources, Go SDK, build environment, commands, and both
new binaries. Do not modify production files or frozen binaries. Select exactly
one control per process via a validated environment value. A `//go:noinline`
region helper performs exactly 1,024 calls to backend.Execute with the same
context, op, shapes, seeds, and discarded outputs as the existing callback.
Setup and one dispatch/pool warmup occur in a different helper before accounting.
This duplicates only the loop boundary; it is site-attribution instrumentation,
not a benchmark or latency result.

Set `GODEBUG=memprofilerate=1` in the process environment, rather than changing
MemProfileRate after initialization, and verify the value remains 1. Preallocate
fixed-capacity pre/post `[]runtime.MemProfileRecord` buffers before warmup; use
`inuseZero=true` and fail rather than resize if capacity is insufficient.

Per invocation, the fixed order is: setup; one warmup; runtime.GC; raw pre
MemProfile snapshot; ReadMemStats S; 1,024 region calls; ReadMemStats E
immediately; runtime.GC; raw post snapshot; then symbolization/JSON and one
standard post-run `allocs.pprof`. Also retain MemStats immediately before the
post snapshot and after serialization so GC/profile-output tail allocations are
visible separately. The primary exact totals are E.TotalAlloc-S.TotalAlloc and
E.Mallocs-S.Mallocs. Compute raw profile deltas within the process by key
`object-size + raw PC stack`, retain alloc/free objects and bytes for every key,
then normalize to function/file/line for cross-binary comparison. Report all
sites, plus subtotals whose stack contains the noinline region helper and all
other Go/runtime stacks. The final pprof is corroborative and cumulative, not a
region delta.

Prospective command templates (not run here), after review, are:

    PATH=/private/tmp/goai-go1271-j1cLvq/go/bin:$PATH GOCACHE=/private/tmp/gocache-goai GOMODCACHE=/private/tmp/goai-go1271-j1cLvq/modcache GOTOOLCHAIN=local CGO_ENABLED=0 GOEXPERIMENT=simd go test -c ./backend/cpu -o <pin-specific-binary>
    GODEBUG=memprofilerate=1 GOMAXPROCS=<1-or-12> GOAI_ALLOC_SITE_DIAGNOSTIC=softplus GOAI_ALLOC_SITE_OUT=<unique-run-directory> <pin-specific-binary> -test.run '^TestCPUControlAllocationSites$' -test.count=1 -test.v

The harness must reject a reused/nonempty output directory, and the runner must
expand and retain each literal command. It must not also pass
`-test.memprofilerate`, which would change the rate later in testing.M.before.

Matrix. Primary scope is Softplus only because it is the one repeated strong
old/V2 direction. Run mandatory phases old-old then old/V2, campaigns 1..3,
pairs 1..7, at GOMAXPROCS 1 and 12. Odd campaigns order process counts 1 then 12;
even campaigns reverse. Arm A precedes B when pair+campaign is even, otherwise B
precedes A. One invocation runs one arm/process-count/control only, serialized
with no owned build/test/profile/benchmark overlap. Preserve ordinary-background
disclosure. The matrix is 3 campaigns * 7 pairs * 2 process counts * 2 arms =
84 invocations per phase, 168 total. Do not add Sigmoid or SiLU
after looking at results; they require a separately preregistered extension.

Retain per invocation: all pins/order metadata; exit/PASS; N/procs/rate; S/E and
tail MemStats counters; raw pre/post records including PCs/object size and all
four profile counts; normalized site deltas; cumulative allocs.pprof; stdout and
stderr. Retain every paired B-minus-A exact-total and per-site delta, ranges,
medians, and sign counts. P-values, if shown, are descriptive only.

Completeness/invalidity. A phase is unanalyzable if any planned invocation is
missing/replaced, fails, uses wrong pin/order/N/procs/rate, overflows the fixed
profile buffer, cannot decode/symbolize its records/profile, has decreasing
cumulative allocation fields for a same-process site, or violates exact integer
arithmetic. Preserve invalid runs; never rerun silently. Profile-site sums need
not equal MemStats and are reported with their difference, not used as an
invalidity criterion.

Interpretation. Old/old site movement establishes what this instrumented pipeline
can produce without a binary difference; it is not a subtractable noise floor.
A repeatable normalized old/V2 site delta supports candidate association only in
the rate-1 profiled harness. It does not show that GELU caused it, that the same
delta exists unprofiled, or that a runtime/background stack was initiated by OS
activity. Absence of a delta does not establish equivalence. No nonsignificant
p-value, profile attribution, or exact-total result can rescore V2, relax a gate,
or justify a scheduling/runtime optimization without a separate proposal.
