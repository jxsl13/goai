## MOE-002
WHERE auxiliary-loss-free MoE routing is enabled, the router SHALL use the paper affinity exactly: s=sigmoid(routerLogit), TopK ranks s+b, and combine weights derive from s with b absent, renormalizing only after selection.

Rationale: The unoptioned Mixtral route stays softmax. The bias is control state outside Params and autograd and updates only through the explicit per-batch sign rule. Required gates include a decisive case where sigmoid(logit)+b and logit+b select different experts, plus a router finite-difference gradient check. Migrated from cavekit SPEC.md V32.

## LOSS-002
The EagleLoss feature regression SHALL compute mean SmoothL1 with beta=1 against the next feature, then add 0.1 times token cross-entropy, never substituting an MSE proxy.

Rationale: Required decisive gates: the abs(d)>1 linear branch, a full-head finite-difference gradient check, and lossless generation at an arbitrary head. Migrated from cavekit SPEC.md V33.

## OPT-003
WHERE Q-GaLore is selected, the optimizer SHALL apply all four deltas: block-wise INT8 moments, packed affine INT4 projection, adaptive SVD gap at 0.4/2/5, and INT8 weights re-quantized by unbiased stochastic rounding.

Rationale: QuantBits 0 or 64 disables all four and must stay bit-identical to GaLore across both projection sides and full rank. No end-to-end weight-memory claim is permitted while the public Tensor keeps a float execution mirror. Migrated from cavekit SPEC.md V34.

## OPT-004
WHERE APOLLO is selected, the optimizer SHALL persist only a two-word projection seed, low-rank moments and a limiter scalar, never retaining the O(r*min(m,n)) Gaussian projection matrix.

Rationale: P regenerates bit-identically from the seed on every use and changes only when the gap replaces that seed; reseeding preserves the moment buffers. Required gates cover both projection sides, seed regeneration equality, gap reseed difference, state shape, convergence and trajectory determinism. Migrated from cavekit SPEC.md V35.

## DIVIDE-THE-SERIAL-SHARE-BY-THE-FORK-COUNT-001
WHEN estimating the payoff of banding an inner loop that sits under a long sequential outer loop, the estimate SHALL divide the serial share by the number of outer iterations, because each one pays its own fork, and expect far less than the share alone promises.

Rationale: Measured on the AQLM encoders Gauss-Jordan solve. A parallel profile showed the solve running entirely on the main goroutine while workers waited in pthread_cond_wait, and it accounted for about 40 percent of the encodes wall clock. Banding the elimination step - which is bit-identical, since each row writes only its own row and reads a pivot row nobody writes - returned only 6.6 percent end to end: BenchmarkEncodeAQLM_256x256 167.8 to 156.7 ms and _128x512 167.6 to 156.0 ms, with HQQuantize flat as a control. The gap is the fork count. The system is 512 wide, so there are about 500 pivot steps and each one spawns its own workers. Four gate constants were measured, 1<<12 1<<14 1<<16 and 1<<18, and none recovered the difference; 1<<14 was best and 1<<18 was effectively serial. Gated by two tests: a residual oracle that solves and checks A times X against B, blind to how the elimination is organized, and a bit-exact digest of the encoders codes and codebooks across three shapes including M=3. Folded into the PS3040 guidance with a floor asserting it reaches the reader. PS3040 also gained a suppression it needed: applying it leaves a below-gate arm that is a plain duplicated loop, which is the checks own advice and also exactly the shape it looks for, so it reported the site it had just been used to fix.

## BAND-A-NEST-THAT-WRITES-THROUGH-A-DERIVED-BASE-001
WHEN a serial nest writes only through a base hoisted from the outermost loop variable, such as obase := b*out then dst[obase+j], the owner of that nest SHALL band the outer loop through the packages fan-out helper and gate the change with BOTH a bit-exact digest and -race.

Rationale: Measured on the KAN fused spline, which was 84 percent of its layers benchmark and fully serial while buildBasis, the other half of the same forward, had fanned out since it was written. Banding the batch: BenchmarkKAN_256x256 67.28 to 10.90 ms, minus 83.8 percent, a 6.2x on 12 cores, and _128x128 8.94 to 1.88 ms, minus 79.0 percent, with HQQuantize flat as a control. Bit-identical because a band never splits a row, so each rows accumulation over the input dimension keeps the same ascending order. THE SHAPE IS PS3034s BLIND SPOT and it has now cost two finds: PS3034 asks whether each write NAMES the outermost loop variable, and a nest that hoists its row offset into a local does not. The other miss was the GGUF weight transpose, also 84 percent of its own benchmark at one core, also large once banded at minus 66.3 percent. Widening PS3034 was tried and reverted - following derived names broke three of its own fixtures and took its tree-wide count from 23 to 33 without flagging the transpose - so a separate narrow check, PS3059, is what shipped, with 22 candidates tree-wide. WHICH GATE EARNS ITS KEEP DEPENDS ON THE DESTINATION: here the destination ACCUMULATES, so an overlapping band double-counts and both the digest and -race fire, where a pure permutation writes the same value twice and only -race sees it. That is the contrast with PERMUTATION-BAND-TWO-GATES-001, not a contradiction of it.

## BAND-THE-OUTER-LEVEL-WHEN-THE-INNER-ONE-ALREADY-FORKS-001
WHEN a serial loop iterates over independent items whose per-item work already fans out internally, the owner of that loop SHALL band the item loop too, after hoisting any caller-supplied callback into a serial pass first.

Rationale: Measured on the Muon optimizer step. A parallel profile spent 62 percent of its samples in pthread_cond_wait and pthread_cond_signal and only 32 percent in the matmul, because each parameters five Newton-Schulz iterations fork three times each and nothing overlaps them. Banding the parameter loop returned minus 34.1 percent, 78.79 to 51.92 ms, with HQQuantize flat as a control - and that is with only TWO parameters, far more than two-way overlap of the work alone can explain, because overlapping the parameters also overlaps their fork and join stalls. THE WIN IS THE STALLS, NOT ONLY THE WORK. The gradient callback is called SERIALLY in a first pass on purpose: GradFn belongs to the caller and has never been documented as safe to call from several goroutines, so only what follows it fans out. Bit-identical because each parameter reads its own gradient and writes its own buffers. THE FIRST VERSION OF THE TEST PROVED NOTHING - its three parameters totalled under parallelRows 1<<14 work gate, so both arms ran serially and an overlapping-band mutation passed; a second, larger shape set was added and the mutation now reddens both the digest and the race detector. Encoded as perfscan PS3060, narrowed to loops with an item variable the body uses: a loop with no variable, for range steps, is a sequential refinement almost every time, and without that condition the check reported the Newton-Schulz iteration inside the function it had just been used to fix.

## MERGE-CONSECUTIVE-PASSES-OVER-ONE-BUFFER-001
WHEN three or more sibling loops in one block run over the same bound and all touch the same buffer, the owner of that block SHALL merge them when every index is independent across the loops, so the buffer is touched once instead of once per stage.

Rationale: Measured on the Kimi delta-attention step. Its four per-step passes over the dv by dk state - scale by the decay, S dot k, the rank-1 delta write, S dot q - each streamed the whole state and evicted it before the next began, and every one of them is independent across r. Merged into one pass: BenchmarkKDA_F64_256x128 8.71 to 7.33 ms, minus 15.9 percent, with HQQuantize flat as a control and the race detector clean. BIT-IDENTICAL, and the argument is precise: merging changes only WHEN a row is visited and never how, because each row scale already preceded its own S dot k and its own delta write already preceded its own S dot q in the split form too. THE WIN TRACKS THE BUFFER SIZE, NOT THE LOOP COUNT. The same merge on the 64 by 64 shape, a 32 KB state that is already L1-resident, measured minus 1.8 percent. Report the size dependence or a reader will expect the same 16 percent everywhere. CHECK FOR A CROSS-INDEX DEPENDENCY FIRST - a later loop that needs ALL of an earlier loop output cannot merge. Encoded as perfscan PS3066, 4 candidates tree-wide; its base-collection had to count SLICE expressions as well as index expressions, because a stage that hands one row to a helper writes S[r*dk : r*dk+dk] and counting only index expressions made the check silent on the very step it was written from.
